# Statistical-analysis tables

These files contain statistical summaries only. No borrower-level records are written.

- `statistical_population_waterfall.csv`: exclusions between the approved fixed-horizon cohort and the statistical complete-case population.
- `statistical_model_summary.csv`: outcome, population, model size, convergence and covariance details.
- `continuous_adjusted_effects.csv`: adjusted risks and confidence intervals at selected continuous-variable values.
- `continuous_nonlinearity_tests.csv`: likelihood-ratio checks comparing spline and linear forms.
- `categorical_adjusted_effects.csv`: pooled, vintage-standardised and mix-adjusted category results.
- `processing_method_overlap.csv`: time-overlap eligibility for named processing-method comparisons.
- `processing_method_pairwise_overlap_adjusted.csv`: overlap-weighted named-method comparisons and effective sample sizes.
- `term_96_120_adjusted_sensitivity.csv`: adjusted term results under the two approved horizons on the same loans.
- `historical_context_comparison.csv`: comparable pooled segment results for FY2001+ and FY2010+.
- `factor_conclusion_summary.csv`: Interpretation of each major factor after statistical adjustment.
- `multiple_comparison_summary.csv`: industry and state multiplicity results with practical and temporal screens.
